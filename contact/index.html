The story behind UI-Utils

UI-Utils was created by Coderx Gamer and MrBreakNFix in 2022, when we met on an anarchy server, both reverse-engineering how its plugins handled inventory packets. What started as a pair of debugging tools grew into UI-Utils: a client-side toolkit for auditing server and plugin behavior.
