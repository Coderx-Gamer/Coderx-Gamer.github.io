Privacy policy

Effective 13 August 2026

At UI-Utils.com (“we”, “our”, “us”), your privacy is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal data when you visit our website [https://ui-utils.com] (“Website”), how we comply with data protection regulations like the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and your rights regarding your information.

By using our Website, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information: We may collect personal information when you voluntarily provide it, such as when you sign up for a newsletter, contact us, or use any interactive features on our Website. This can include your name, email address, and other relevant details.

1.2 Non-Personal Information: We automatically collect non-personal information when you visit our Website, including your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited on our Website. This data is primarily used for analytical and performance improvement purposes.

1.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (e.g., web beacons) to collect information about how you use our Website. These technologies help us analyze trends, administer the site, track users’ movement around the site, and gather demographic information.

Cookies may be placed by us or by third-party partners such as Google AdSense. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set, and you can withdraw or change that choice at any time through the consent notice. You can also control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may limit your ability to use some features of our Website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve the functionality of our Website and services.
  • To communicate with you about your use of the Website or respond to your inquiries.
  • To provide personalized advertising (where applicable) and analyze the performance of ads on our Website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. However, we may share your non-personal information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or analytical purposes.

3.1 Third-Party Services: We use third-party services such as Google AdSense and Google Analytics:

  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display personalized ads. These ads are served based on your interests and browsing activity. You can opt-out of personalized ads by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Visit counter: We count page visits, along with any referral parameters present in the page URL, using a Google Apps Script endpoint. This is aggregate traffic data and is not used to identify you. You can learn more about Google's handling of data by visiting Google's Privacy Policy.

4. GDPR Compliance (General Data Protection Regulation)

If you are a resident of the European Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request corrections to any personal data we hold about you that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that we delete your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: You can request that we limit the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the contact details provided at the end of this Privacy Policy.

5. CCPA Compliance (California Consumer Privacy Act)

If you are a resident of California, you have the following rights under the CCPA:

  • Right to Know: You can request to know what personal data we have collected about you, including the categories and specific pieces of data.
  • Right to Delete: You can request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can opt-out of the sale of your personal data to third parties (although we do not sell your personal information).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at mrbreaknfix@gmail.com. We will verify your request before processing it.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to serve ads. In regions where consent is required, personalized ads are only served after you agree to them; you can decline and still use the site. You can manage or change your choice at any time through the consent notice, or through your browser settings.

7. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Your personal information may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside of your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

8. Your Rights and Choices

See the section on GDPR and CCPA for your rights to access, correct, delete, or opt out of personal data collection.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our Website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete that information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.
